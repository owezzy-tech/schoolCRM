# Grades 1–3: KICD preview summaries

These original summaries describe sampled preview pages, not the complete curriculum. Page references use the PDF viewer’s page numbers; printed page numbers can differ. Publication notes do not establish that a revision is approved for retrieval.

The source documents combine Grades 1–3. The reviewed pages generally concern introductory scope and the earliest grade section; no separate Grade 2 or Grade 3 progression is inferred. The Creative Activities document also includes indigenous-language material that was not separately reviewed here.
