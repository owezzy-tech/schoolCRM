## What these summaries cover

The summaries are written in original prose from public preview text and one visual page review. They do not reproduce the source documents. Each entry records the pages reviewed and links to the KICD publication page and the original preview in `index.jsonl`. Tables use PDF viewer page numbers, not printed page numbers. Only sampled portions of those pages were reviewed.

Most entries cover the document identity, publication details, subject overview or early unit. They do not establish complete strand coverage, lesson allocations, assessment rules, term sequencing or progression. PP1/PP2 coverage is limited to early language sections; Grades 1–3 source documents are combined and should not be mistaken for independent reviews of each grade.

All entries remain `unreviewed` for retrieval approval. These summaries are planning aids, not replacements for authorised curriculum originals or validated source citations in generated lesson plans. They do not resolve `schoolCRM-o58.2.2` or complete curriculum ingestion.
